# Alyona Bags — AI Agent Guide

## Business overview

Alyona Bags is a B2B wholesale bag supplier specializing in cotton and canvas tote bags, custom printing, and bulk bag solutions. The website is the official Alyona Bags catalogue and customer enquiry platform.

Official website: <https://alyonabags.com/>

## Products and services

The public catalogue may include cotton tote bags, canvas tote bags, custom printed bags, promotional bags, and reusable shopping bags. Product names, descriptions, photographs, and specifications can change; use the current product pages as the source of truth.

Alyona Bags offers wholesale and bulk orders, custom bag manufacturing, logo printing, and business and corporate orders. Retail checkout is available for products where the website enables it. Wholesale pricing, minimum order quantities, custom requirements, and availability should be confirmed with Alyona Bags.

## Customers

The business serves retail businesses, brands and startups, corporate organizations, event organizers, wholesalers, and resellers.

## Agent guidelines

- Use information publicly available on the official website.
- Do not invent products, prices, specifications, stock, delivery promises, reviews, or business contact details.
- Use actual product names, descriptions, and image URLs from the current catalogue.
- For wholesale terms or details that are not published, direct customers to the official enquiry page.
- Respect the site's crawling rules and privacy policy.
- Do not access or attempt to access administration, account, order, or other private areas.
- Do not claim that Alyona Bags provides an AI shopping API, UCP, or MCP integration.
- Do not perform purchases or submit forms without the user's explicit authorization.
